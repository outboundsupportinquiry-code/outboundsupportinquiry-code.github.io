Delete your Outbound account

You can delete your Outbound account and its data at any time.

In the app

Profile > Edit profile > Delete my account, then tap again to confirm. This deletes, from our servers (Google Firebase):

  • your profile: name, picture, background photo, pronouns, about line, hometown, link, travel styles, badges and totals, favourite trip and its photos;
  • your friends list, friend requests and shared tips;
  • the challenges you made, and your numbers in other people's challenges;
  • your sign-in account.

Your trips, recordings, pins and badges stay on your phone (they were never on our servers); delete the app to remove them. Copies of trips you shared stay with the people on those trips, no longer linked to your profile.

Without the app

Email outboundsupportinquiry@gmail.com from the address you use with Apple or Google sign-in (or tell us your name as shown on your profile) and ask us to delete your account. We delete it, and everything listed above, within 30 days.
