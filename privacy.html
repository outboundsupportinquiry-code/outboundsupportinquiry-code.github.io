Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026. Version 1.

Outbound is built so that your information stays with you. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not track you. What leaves your device is only what you choose to share: a profile and friends list if you sign in, tips you share, and trips you plan with the people on them. This policy explains what the App uses and where it is kept.

Summary

  • Your location, trips, saved places and health figures are processed on your device.
  • We run no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not build profiles.
  • We never sell or share personal information with anyone.
  • Health data is read only, used only to show your activity in the App, never written, shared, used for advertising or marketing, or stored in iCloud.
  • Backups go to your own iCloud (iOS) or Google account (Android), not to us.

Network requests

The App downloads map content (terrain, imagery, map tiles) and, where available, weather or closure information. These requests necessarily reach the servers that provide that content and include standard technical information (such as your IP address and which map area is requested). They do not include your identity, trips or health data. Content providers include the ones listed in the App under "Data sources and licences".

Weather. When you open Weather and conditions with online weather on, the App asks the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) for the forecast and alerts at your location rounded to about 1 km, and nothing else about you. You can turn online weather off in that screen; sunrise and sunset are always worked out on your device.

Air quality and park alerts. When online weather is on, the App asks Outbound's own server for air quality near you (your position rounded to about 10 km) and, inside a national park the App has a map for, the Park Service's alerts for that park. Our server passes on only that rounded position or the park's code – never your IP address or anything else about you – to the U.S. EPA's AirNow and the National Park Service, and keeps no logs.

Trips planned together. When a trip has people invited to it, each phone uploads its copy of the trip to Google Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase) when you leave the App and downloads the others' copies when you open it, so everyone sees the latest plans without sending anything. Only the trip itself is sent: no location, health data, photos or account. A trip of your own that nobody else is on is never uploaded. Google's privacy policy applies to Firebase.

Profiles, friends, tips and challenges. These are optional and need you to sign in with Apple or Google (Firebase Authentication receives the sign-in token from Apple or Google; the App does not see your password). Only what is listed in the table above is uploaded; your location, trips, recordings and health data are not, except the single number a challenge you joined counts. Sign out from Profile > Edit profile. Delete your account there too (Profile > Edit profile > Delete my account): it removes your profile, pictures, friends list, friend requests, tips, the challenges you made and your numbers in others' challenges from Firebase, and your sign-in account; your trips, recordings and badges stay on your device. Trips you share stay with the people on them, no longer linked to your profile, and your entry in other people's friends lists stops showing your profile.

Outbound Plus. The App has no purchases today. If Plus is offered later and you buy it, Apple or Google handle the payment; the App only learns which Plus you have and until when, keeps that on your device, and puts the end date on the trips you host so the people on them are covered. We never see your payment details.

Discoveries. While you drive with directions or record a drive (you can turn this off), and when you keep a trip offline, the App asks Wikipedia (the Wikimedia Foundation) for articles about places near you or along the trip, sending a position rounded to about 1 km and nothing else about you. Wikimedia's privacy policy applies.

Search and directions. With a signal, what you type in a search box is also sent to a place search, with the point to search around (your position or the map's centre, rounded to about 1 km) and nothing else about you: on iPhone to Apple Maps (Apple's privacy policy applies), on Android to the phone's own place search (Google's, through Android; Google's privacy policy applies), and only when those find nothing (or on other devices) to Photon, the OpenStreetMap search run by komoot. When you ask for directions with a signal, the App downloads the road data for the drive from Outbound's public download page; that request names only the map areas, never the destination. Without a signal, search and directions use only the data on your device.

Links to other services. A place's card has links to look it up on Yelp, Google Maps or Apple Maps (for reviews, hours and photos, which the App does not hold). Nothing is sent until you tap one; then your browser or that app opens the service's page with the place's name and address or position, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Your own ratings of places are never sent.

Health data

If you connect Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), the App reads step count and walking + running distance to show your activity. The App:

  • never writes to your health data;
  • never shares health data with third parties or uses it for advertising, marketing or data mining;
  • never stores health data in iCloud or any cloud service;
  • only reads it when you view activity or trip statistics.

You can remove the App's access at any time in Settings > Health (iOS) or in Health Connect (Android).

Purchases

The App has no purchases. If any are offered later, Apple or Google will handle them entirely: we would receive no payment details, and the App would only learn what was bought.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has made a profile, we delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Location, health and photo access can be allowed or removed at any time in your device settings.
  • Deleting the App deletes its data on the device; backups can be removed from your iCloud or Google account settings.
  • Your profile, tips and challenges are stored with Firebase only while you use those features; you can change or remove them in the App, delete your account and its data from Profile > Edit profile > Delete my account, or ask us to delete them. Apart from that, we do not collect personal information, so there is nothing else held by us to access, correct or delete. You may contact us with any questions. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have rights regarding personal information; we do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the App will show the new version and the date it takes effect.
