Outbound support

Outbound plans road trips and hikes with friends, maps them offline and keeps your travel history, with no ads and no tracking.

Help

  • Join a friend's trip: open their invite link, or in Outbound tap Trips > + > Join a trip.
  • Offline maps: Settings > Offline maps. Trips download what they need when you keep them offline.
  • Travel history: Profile > Travel history reads where your photos were taken and imports Google Maps Timeline, KML and GPX files.
  • Outbound Plus: Settings > Outbound Plus. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account; Restore purchases brings Plus back on a new phone.
  • Delete your account: see [Delete your account](delete-account.html).
